CORE EXPERTISE

AML & Financial Crime Skills

Transaction Monitoring

Review transaction activity, identify unusual patterns, and flag potentially suspicious behavior for further investigation to support timely risk detection.

Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

Apply AML principles when reviewing financial activity, recognizing potential red flags, and escalating concerns in accordance with established procedures.

Know Your Customer (KYC)

Verify customer information against applicable requirements to support reliable identity checks and reduce the risk of identity-related fraud.

Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

Review customer information and relevant risk factors to support informed customer assessments and appropriate due diligence decisions.

Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

Identify cases that may require deeper scrutiny, assess additional risk information, and support appropriate escalation of higher-risk relationships.

Fraud Investigation & Detection

Investigate potentially unauthorized transactions, assess fraud indicators, and document findings to support accurate case decisions and reduce exposure to payment fraud.

Suspicious Activity Identification

Detect unusual transaction patterns and account behavior, distinguish potential red flags from routine activity, and identify cases requiring further review.

Risk Assessment

Evaluate available evidence and relevant risk indicators to prioritize cases, guide investigation depth, and support consistent risk-based decisions.

Sanctions & PEP Screening

Review relevant screening results for potential sanctions or politically exposed person matches and identify cases that may require verification or escalation.

Adverse Media Screening

Assess relevant negative news for potential financial crime or reputational risk indicators to support customer risk reviews and further due diligence.

Analytical Thinking

Connect information across transactions, accounts, and case records to identify patterns, investigate inconsistencies, and reach evidence-based conclusions.

Attention to Detail

Validate key information, maintain accurate case documentation, and follow investigation procedures to help minimize errors and support quality standards.
